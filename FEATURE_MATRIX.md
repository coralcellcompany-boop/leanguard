## AI tools inside the app

### 1. Lean Coach chat

Answers questions about the user's plan and recent logged data. It should cite the relevant in-app facts it used, such as “2 of 3 workouts completed” or “protein target met on 4 of 7 days.”

### 2. Weekly Muscle Protection Review

Combines weight-loss pace, strength trend, workout completion, activity, and protein consistency into a short summary with no more than three next actions.

### 3. Smart Plan Adapter

Suggests changes to exercise load, reps, weekly volume, substitutions, or cardio volume. The user must approve changes before the plan is updated.

### 4. Protein Planner

Creates protein-first meal and snack suggestions based on remaining target, dietary preferences, allergies, time, and appetite level. It should not prescribe restrictive diets.

### 5. Progress Signal Detector

Detects patterns such as fast weight loss plus declining strength, repeated missed workouts, plateaued lifts, low step consistency, or protein shortfalls. It explains the evidence and suggests a conservative action.

### 6. Optional GLP-1 Support Assistant

Focuses on routine support: hydration, protein-first eating, energy-aware exercise, and preparation for a clinician conversation. It never advises starting, stopping, or changing medication.

## Recommended Pro conversion moments

- After completing the starter plan and requesting a new personalized phase.
- When opening a locked AI weekly insight.
- When asking the AI Coach after the free monthly allowance is used.
- When requesting an automatic plan adjustment.
- When viewing progress beyond four weeks.
- When enabling advanced GLP-1 support or full health-data correlations.

Never interrupt an active workout with a paywall. Show the upgrade after completion or when the user explicitly opens a locked Pro feature.
